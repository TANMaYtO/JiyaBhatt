# Art direction — Jiyaa Bhatt

## Direction
An editorial writing portfolio with a literary/gothic undertone: black ink, pale lilac, lavender linework, and restrained violet highlights borrowed from the typewriter and neural artwork. It should feel composed, typographic, and human rather than a stack of rounded UI cards.

## Palette
- Ink Black: `#08070c`
- Deep Plum Panels: `#17121f`
- Soft Ink / Secondary: `#100d16`
- Pale Lilac (Readable Paper): `#eee7fa`
- Lilac Accent: `#a987e8`
- Bright Lavender: `#c4a8ff`
- Soft Lavender: `#b69aff`
- Muted Type: `#b9accd`
- Deep Lilac / Rules: `rgba(207, 192, 232, 0.19)`

## Typography
- Instrument Serif for italic editorial emphasis and literary moments
- DM Sans for name, headlines, and readable body text
- DM Mono for index numbers, folio tags, metadata, rules, and technical annotations

Fonts are progressive enhancement; system fallbacks remain in place.

## Image handling
The blue typewriter/television artwork serves as an intentional small offset inset in the hero. The neural branches, open books, and paper butterflies artwork supports the featured AI/internet-culture article. The 8 individual blog thumbnails extracted from the Canva design represent each of Jiyaa's published articles in the Writing Archive. The portrait is a purpose-built vector placeholder until Jiyaa supplies her real photo.

## Original graphic motifs
- `celestial-ornament.svg`: fine-line geometric ornament, used as a quiet watermark and contact-section detail
- `branch-ink.svg`: branching technical diagram for the process section
- `paper-moth.svg`: delicate line-art paper moth/book-page motif, used in the hero and About section
- `folio-grid.svg`: editorial grid/celestial treatment for the writing archive background
- `article-*.png/jpg`: 8 genuine blog thumbnails for the writing archive
- `favicon.svg`: browser tab icon

## Motion principles
- Name and role are introduced in a short sequence; main copy remains immediately legible
- Scroll reveal effects are observational, not scroll-jacking
- Reading progress and nav compaction respond to scroll position
- A restrained crosshair cursor and a few-pixel artwork parallax apply only on fine-pointer devices
- Hover transitions reveal a rule, arrow, or image detail rather than applying decorative movement to every object
- `prefers-reduced-motion` disables/reduces nonessential motion

## Content integrity
All 8 writing archive entries represent real, verified published articles by Jiyaa Bhatt on Newz24India and Medium. Real canonical URLs connect directly to the published pieces. No invented metrics, employers, testimonials, or credentials are included.
